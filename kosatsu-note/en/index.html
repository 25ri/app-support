From a question to an idea

  • Keep notes for an episode or an entire work, with optional timestamps, page numbers, people and tags.
  • Search the full text of saved notes, titles, people and tags. Filter by work, episode or unresolved questions.
  • Connect notes as related, evidence, counterevidence, or payoff / resolution. Explore them on a map.
  • Attach up to 10 reference photos per note and read them alongside your writing.
  • Split plain text into notes manually or with optional on-device AI, then review before importing.
  • On supported devices, use Apple Intelligence to suggest connections. Save only the suggestions you choose.
  • Share text or Markdown, sync with iCloud if enabled, and keep complete JSON backups.

English and Japanese

Version 1.11.0 and later follow the app language selected in iOS, including the guide and practice notebook. Your saved writing is never translated when you change the interface language. Language settings and guided practice.

Your notebook lives on your device

No app account is required to write, organize or search notes. iCloud sync and automatic backup are separate optional features, both off by default.

Restoring after reinstalling requires a completed backup, the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive. Read the backup and restore guide before removing the app.
