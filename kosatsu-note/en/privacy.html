Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy describes information handling in Theory Notes (考察ノート), an iPhone and iPad app provided by YUTA NIGORIKAWA, and on this support website.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores work and theme names, episodes, note text, titles, timestamps or page numbers, classifications, people, tags, connections, viewing or reading status, cover images, reference photos, drafts and trash on your device to support writing, organization, search and restoration.

The app has no separate account registration, advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives notebooks and does not automatically receive your note text, photos or backups.

On-device AI and text import

AI connection suggestions and text splitting use Apple Intelligence on a supported device and run locally. Note bodies and titles used for comparison, and text you import, are not sent to the developer's server or an external AI service. Beginning excerpts are used when comparing long notes. Suggestions do not create connections until you review and save your choices.

When you start an import by sharing or pasting, the received plain text and unsaved candidates are kept on the device so you can resume. AI splitting preserves original wording and line breaks. You can review the text and destination before saving. Changing between Japanese and English does not translate your saved writing.

2. Optional iCloud backup and sync

Automatic iCloud backup is off by default. When explicitly enabled, complete notebook snapshots, including cover images, reference photos, drafts and trash, are sent to private app-specific iCloud storage associated with your Apple Account.

The notebook remains stored locally. Backup snapshots do not merge edits from different devices. The developer has no feature for viewing your private backups.

Separately, when you explicitly enable iCloud sync on each device, the notebook, including images, drafts and trash, is sent to your Apple Account's private CloudKit storage to merge changes between devices. Conflicts stop automatic overwriting. Both versions are retained locally before applying your chosen version. Turning sync off does not delete saved data.

New backup generations are created only when content changes. The latest five generations from this installation, plus the last generation for each of the latest seven days with a backup, are kept. Older generations are cleaned up after a new upload is confirmed. Legacy-format backups and those from other installations are not automatically removed. You can review dates and counts before choosing a generation to restore.

Backup and restore require iCloud Drive, a network connection and sufficient iCloud space. Restoring after reinstalling requires the same Apple Account and a completed backup. Pending or uploading changes are not guaranteed to be recoverable.

Empty initial data does not automatically overwrite an existing backup. Restoring replaces all current data and keeps the previous data locally. Stopping automatic backup keeps existing backups; removing the app also leaves completed iCloud backups.

To remove a backup, select it in the app and confirm the action to stop automatic backup and delete it. Local notes and other backups remain. Apple's information handling also applies; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Reference photos

Images you select, capture or paste are attached to notes in the app. Using the camera requires iOS permission. Images are resized as JPEGs while preserving readability; associated metadata such as location and capture time is not retained. The app does not write images to Photos.

Reference photos are included in local and iCloud backups and in iCloud sync when enabled. They are excluded from text exports and are not sent directly through the share sheet.

Only when you choose Copy is a saved photo placed on this device's clipboard, with transfer through Universal Clipboard disabled. Once pasted, handling is governed by the destination app or service. Please check the image owner's rights and applicable usage terms.

4. Official pages and cover images

Opening an official site or character chart uses the standard in-app Safari browser and connects to that site and any external services it loads. Selecting a visible page area as a cover uses a temporary web view. Those sites may process IP addresses, browser information and cookies under their own policies.

Apple's Link Presentation may retrieve a URL's shared image and metadata when you register an official URL. This also contacts the destination site. Your note text is not sent to the image source.

Retrieved or selected cover images are stored on the device and used for normal lists. They are included in local backups and in iCloud backup or sync when enabled. Images are not automatically published to this support website.

All works and images belong to their rights holders. Check permissions and site terms before using images. The app is not an official service of an author, broadcaster, production company or streaming provider.

5. Exporting, sharing and deleting

When you choose text or Markdown export or sharing, the system save or share interface passes the previewed text to the destination you select. You can choose the body only or include selected fields. Reference photos, drafts and trash are excluded.

A local JSON backup includes the complete notebook, including cover images, reference photos, drafts and trash. Exported files and shared content are then managed by the chosen destination service or recipient.

Notes can be restored or permanently deleted from the app's trash. Removing the app deletes its local app data, including data retained before restoration. iCloud data and exported files require separate management; removing the app does not delete saved iCloud backups.

6. This support website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no developer-added analytics tags, advertising, contact forms, external fonts or tracking cookies. Hosting-related information such as IP addresses is also handled by GitHub.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

7. Support inquiries and policy changes

If you email support, your sender address and the information in your message are used to respond and investigate the issue. Your entire notebook or complete backup is not automatically collected.

For privacy questions, contact 25ri.apps.studio@gmail.com.

This policy and its update date are reviewed when app features or information handling change.
