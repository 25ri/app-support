Guide & support

Theory Notes is a notebook for the things you notice in stories and themes. Your notes are stored on your iPhone or iPad. iCloud sync and backup are optional.

For version 1.11.0 · Updated November 14, 2026. Earlier versions may look different. English support requires version 1.11.0 or later.

Language and guided practice

The app follows the language selected in iOS and displays English or Japanese. There is no language switch inside the app. To choose a language for this app, open its entry in iOS Settings and use the language option. If the option is missing, add the language under Language & Region first.

The interface, guide, practice notebook and AI guidance use the selected language. Changing it does not translate or change your saved work names, note text, people, tags or connections.

Open Settings → Try the App to practice adding a work, writing notes, resolving a question, recording a payoff and reviewing notes. Practice content stays separate from your own notebook.

Write and review notes

  1. Add a work and choose its type: TV series, film, anime, novel, manga, game, stage production, event or theme, or other. Seasons, episodes and official links are optional.
  2. Choose Write a note in the Library. Tap the destination at the top of the editor to change the episode or use a work-wide note. Film notes belong to the main feature.
  3. Text saves automatically. Use Save and continue for another new note, or Done to finish a new or existing note and return. Unfinished input can be resumed from drafts.
  4. Review notes in the Notes tab with search and filters. Opening a work or episode's note list applies its context. Show all clears the search and filters.

Optional details and organization

A title is optional. Settings → Note Input Settings controls which fields appear in the editor and which are grouped under details. People, tags, note type, reference photos, links and a playback position or page number can be added when needed. Changing these display settings keeps saved information.

Use page numbers for novels and manga. Other notes can use a playback position where appropriate. Pinning and official links are in the details. You can also refer to other notes and return to your writing.

The season and episode management screen lets you add, rename and reorder seasons or episodes. Returning the planned episode count to an unspecified value does not delete existing episodes or notes. Edit a person's name and notes together from the work's people and tags screen. While selecting people for a note, use the information button to open their notes, then choose Edit person. Renaming a person keeps their note associations.

iPad and landscape

On a wide iPad screen, the work or note list appears beside the detail. Use the button at the top left of the list to hide it, then the button at the top left of the detail to show it again. Search text, filters and the selected note are retained. Reference notes open beside your writing when there is enough space. iPhone also supports portrait and both landscape orientations.

In settings and the note list, tap a switch's label or surrounding row space as well as the switch itself to change its setting.

Cover images and official pages

Retrieve an official URL's shared image or select a visible area of the page as the work's image. Images are stored on the device. Official sites and character charts open in the standard in-app Safari browser.

Search

The Notes tab searches the entire saved note body, titles, people and tags. Work, episode and type filters, as well as the option to hide resolved notes, also affect results. Use Show all to clear the search and filters.

Link notes also searches entire note bodies, but only among saved notes in the same work. The source note itself, drafts, additional details and notes in the trash are excluded. Episode filters and the work-wide-only option can narrow results further. Existing connections show their status; the same connection cannot be saved twice.

In version 1.11.0, searching places the result count and candidates first. You can select a note while the keyboard is open. Use Clear search and episode filters to reset the conditions. Open Settings at the top right of the linking screen to change the relationship or direction.

Connections and the map

In 1.13.0, Write a new note and link opens the editor with its counterpart already selected. Saving the text also saves the connection. Drafts retain the counterpart, relationship, direction and optional connection note if you stop writing. Try the same flow in the guided practice notebook. A Related connection does not automatically resolve a question.

  1. Open Link notes in a note's connections section.
  2. The initial relationship is Related. Change it to evidence, counterevidence or payoff / resolution as needed. In 1.13.0, connections accept any note type and remain when you change a note’s type. Choose payoff / resolution only when you want to give the connection that meaning.
  3. Select one or more notes, then confirm the relationship, direction and selection count before saving. Notes can belong to different episodes of the same work. An additional comment is optional.

For a question, use Record Resolution. For a clue, use Record Payoff. Choose an existing answer note or write a new one.

On the connection map, drag to move and pinch or use the zoom controls to change scale. Overview fits the visible notes. The return control restores the original note and default scale; without a starting note, use reset. Double-tap a card to open the note.

To create a connection on the map, choose a source card, then search for the target note. Tap a line to review or edit its relationship, direction and comment. The full-text and compact views let you choose how much to show.

AI suggestions

AI requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with the feature enabled and the model ready. Comparisons run on the device. Writing, search and manual linking work without AI.

  1. From a work's Review section, choose the option to find AI suggestions within the work. Choose a comparison scope and tap Find AI suggestions. You can also use Find AI suggestions for this note in the linking screen.
  2. Work-wide suggestions display two notes as a pair. Check their episodes, relationship and direction, then open the review screen to read both notes in full and the proposed reason.
  3. Confirm or change the relationship and direction, and save only what you choose. Showing a suggestion does not create a connection.

Turn automatic searches during writing and saving on or off with Automatically Search for AI Connections in the editor or Settings → Note Input Settings. Even when automatic search is off, Search with AI runs one search using the current text. It also works while editing an existing note. The suggestion controls show progress and results; only the connections you choose are saved.

AI compares beginning excerpts of long notes. This differs from full-text search and may miss connections. If AI is unavailable, fails or finds nothing, search and link notes manually.

Import text

Open text import from the menu at the top right of the Library tab, or choose Theory Notes in another app's share sheet. Import accepts plain text; photos, attachments and rich-text formatting are not imported. It does not recognize text from photos.

  1. Choose the destination work and episode, then paste or share the text.
  2. Use Make Selection a Note to move selected text into a candidate, or Split Here to split at the cursor. On supported devices, AI can propose a split.
  3. Check each candidate's body, type and destination. Edit, split or merge candidates as needed, then import. The original wording and line breaks are preserved.

Resume unfinished text and candidates from Text Waiting to Be Imported. After saving some candidates, the remaining content is kept so that you do not need to import the saved notes again.

Reference photos

Attach up to 10 photos per note using the photo picker, camera or image paste in the editor or details. Use them as a reference while writing; text recognition is not provided.

Photos keep their original proportions. Uniform black bars may be hidden for display only. Tap to enlarge; where bars are hidden, switch to the original photo to see the full image. This does not change the saved image or backups.

Copy places the saved image on this device's clipboard. The app does not save it to Photos or send it directly through the share sheet. Removing an attachment keeps the note body and the original in Photos. Reference photos are included in complete backups and enabled iCloud sync, but not text exports.

Please respect the rights and usage terms of any images you use or copy.

Export and share

Open export and sharing from a note or the menu of a note list filtered by work or episode. Choose the body only or selected fields, check the preview, then share the text or save a file. Field selections are remembered. Plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) are supported.

Only the saved text shown in the preview is exported. Photos, drafts and trash are excluded. A text export is not a complete restorable backup.

Sync, backup and restore

iCloud sync and automatic backup are separate features. Both are off by default. Your notebook is stored locally. Enable each feature explicitly in Settings if you want to use it.

Sync between devices

Enable iCloud sync on each device using the same Apple Account. It includes photos, drafts and trash. Incoming changes wait while you edit. Conflicting changes to the same record require you to choose which content to keep. Turning sync off does not delete saved data.

Automatic backup

Open Settings → Backup → iCloud. Enabling automatic backup stores complete snapshots, including works, episodes, notes, connections, classifications, cover images, reference photos, drafts and trash, in your private app-specific iCloud storage. It requires iCloud Drive, a network connection and sufficient iCloud space.

A new generation is created only when content changes. The app keeps this installation's latest five generations and the last generation for each of the latest seven days with a backup. Older generations are cleaned up after a new upload is confirmed. Legacy-format backups and backups from other installations are not automatically cleaned up.

Check the status and last confirmed upload time. Pending, uploading or failed does not mean the latest content is safely in iCloud. Confirm completion before removing the app. Backup snapshots do not merge edits between devices.

Restore after reinstalling

  1. Sign in with the same Apple Account on your iPhone or iPad and enable iCloud Drive.
  2. Open Settings → Backup → iCloud. Check the date and record counts, then choose a generation. An empty Library also offers an iCloud restore entry.
  3. Review and restore. Restoring replaces the entire current notebook. The previous data is kept locally and can be exported from Settings → Backup → Local.

Only a completed backup can be used after reinstalling. Local-only data and pending uploads are not guaranteed to be recoverable. Empty initial data does not automatically overwrite an existing iCloud backup.

Stop or delete automatic backups

Turning automatic backup off stops future automatic uploads and keeps existing backups. To remove one, select it in the app and confirm the action to stop automatic backup and delete it. Notes on the device and other backups remain.

Local backups

Use Settings → Backup → Local to save a complete JSON backup, including cover images, reference photos, drafts and trash. Select a local backup file to review and restore it. Restoring replaces all current data.

Backups containing page numbers use format v7; others use v6. Older v1, v2, v3, v5 and v6 backups remain readable. Restoring page numbers requires version 1.10.0 or later. Use Export Backup from Before Last Restore on the Local screen to retrieve the previous notebook.

Troubleshooting

A saved note is missing from the list

Check the selected work and episode, filters, search query and trash. Notes without a playback position appear in the untimed group. If a save error appeared, check its message.

A note appears in search but not when linking

Check that it is a saved note in the same work and clear episode filters. The source itself, drafts, additional details and trash are excluded. Existing connections show their status and reason. See Search.

AI suggestions are unavailable

Check device compatibility, Apple Intelligence settings and model readiness. You can always search and link notes manually if AI is unavailable, fails or finds no candidates.

I moved a note to the trash by mistake

Restore it from Settings → Trash. Permanently deleted content requires a backup made before deletion. Restoring a backup replaces the whole notebook.

iCloud backup is not progressing

Check automatic backup settings, your Apple Account, iCloud Drive, network and available iCloud space. Review the in-app status and try backing up now. Do not remove the app while the latest upload is pending.

A cover image or official page does not load

Check the URL and network. A page may lack a shared image, have been removed or restrict access. You can select a visible page area from the work's editor to set a cover image.

Do I need an internet connection?

Local writing, organization and search work offline. Official pages, cover retrieval, iCloud sync, and iCloud backup and restore require a connection.

I cannot share a reference photo

Direct photo sharing and saving to Photos are not provided, and text exports exclude photos. Use Copy in the photo viewer to paste on this device, subject to the image's usage terms.
